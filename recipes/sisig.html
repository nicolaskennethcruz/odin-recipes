Sisig

Description

This sisig is seasoned with calamansi, onions, and chili. It is savory, tangy, and slightly spicy, with a mix of crispy and tender textures.

It is perfect with rice or served as a pulutan.

Ingredients

  • 1kg Pork
  • 500g Real Mayonnaise
  • 3 tbsp Oyster Sauce
  • 2 tbsp Liquid Seasoning
  • 78g Liver Spread
  • 4 tbsp Calamansi Juice
  • 1 Whole White Onion
  • 6 Cloves Garlic
  • 200g Margarine
  • A bit of Pepper
  • A bit of Salt
  • 3 pcs Siling Green

Steps

Pork

  1. Boil the meat in water with pepper, garlic, onion, and salt until tender
  2. Set aside and let it cool completely
  3. Fry over medium heat until golden brown
  4. Let it cool for at least 5 minutes, then fry again over high heat for 1 minute

Sauce

  1. In a bowl, mix oyster sauce, mayonnaise, calamansi juice, liquid seasoning, liver spread, and pepper. Set aside
  2. In a pan with oil and margarine, sauté garlic, onion, and green chili
  3. Add the sauce mixture and simmer for at least 3 minutes
  4. Add the meat and mix well
